## 📌 Project Overview

InterviewAce AI is an AI-powered mock interview platform built to help students, fresh graduates, career changers, and professionals prepare for job interviews through realistic AI-driven practice sessions.

The application allows users to configure interviews based on job role, interview type, difficulty level, experience level, language, and number of questions. Google Gemini AI conducts the interview, evaluates responses, provides detailed feedback, rewrites answers professionally, generates competency scores, and creates a personalized 7-day preparation roadmap.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many students and job seekers struggle with interview preparation because they have limited opportunities to practice realistic interviews and receive personalized feedback. Existing resources usually provide static questions instead of intelligent evaluation.

InterviewAce AI solves this problem by acting as a virtual AI interviewer that conducts realistic mock interviews, evaluates responses, identifies strengths and weaknesses, and recommends a personalized improvement plan.

---

## 💡 Solution

Users can:

- Enter a custom job role
- Paste a complete job description
- Select Technical, HR, or Mixed interviews
- Choose Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced difficulty
- Select experience level
- Choose interview language (English, Urdu, Roman Urdu)
- Configure interview length

The AI then conducts the interview, evaluates every response, generates model answers, calculates competency scores, and creates a tailored learning roadmap.

---

## 🧠 AI System Prompt

The AI is instructed to:

- Act as an experienced interviewer.
- Generate role-specific interview questions.
- Ask one question at a time.
- Adapt questions according to difficulty, interview type, experience level, and language.
- Evaluate every answer professionally.
- Calculate detailed interview scores.
- Rewrite weak answers into stronger professional answers.
- Identify strengths and weaknesses.
- Recommend a personalized preparation plan.
- Provide a final hiring readiness assessment.
